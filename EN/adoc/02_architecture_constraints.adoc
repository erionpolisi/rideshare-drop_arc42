== Architecture Constraints

The constraints below influence design and implementation
decisions for *Drop*. This section lists them and, where applicable,
their motivation. Their identifiers group them by type: technical (TC), organizational
(OC) and conventions (C).

ifdef::arc42help[]
[role="arc42help"]
****
.Contents
Any requirement that constraints software architects in their freedom of design and implementation decisions or decision about the development process. These constraints sometimes go beyond individual systems and are valid for whole organizations and companies.

.Motivation
Architects should know exactly where they are free in their design decisions and where they must adhere to constraints.
Constraints must always be dealt with; they may be negotiable, though.

.Form
Simple tables of constraints with explanations.
If needed you can subdivide them into
technical constraints, organizational and political constraints and
conventions (e.g. programming or versioning guidelines, documentation or naming conventions)


.Further Information

See https://docs.arc42.org/section-2/[Architecture Constraints] in the arc42 documentation.

****
endif::arc42help[]

[cols="1,3,9", options="header"]
|===
| | Constraint | Background and / or motivation

| TC1
| Implementation in Dart and Flutter
| The product is mobile-only, so development uses Dart and the Flutter
framework to support cross-platform deployment for Android and iOS with
minimal platform-specific changes. Firebase is used alongside it because
it integrates efficiently with Flutter for authentication, database and
cloud services.

| TC2
| Use of third-party services
| Building proprietary maps, routing and payment infrastructure is
beyond the scale and budget of this project, so the architecture must
rely on external providers for these core capabilities.

| TC3
| Bilingual system (German and English)
| The system must support both German and English. The company operates
regionally in Austria but plans to expand internationally in the future.

| OC1
| GDPR / DSGVO compliance
| The system processes real-time location data, personal user
information and banking data. Stakeholders require compliance to ensure
data privacy, security and the lawful handling of sensitive information.

| C1
| Documentation in arc42
| arc42 is the company standard for documenting software architecture,
ensuring consistent communication.
|===
