== Risks and Technical Debts

ifdef::arc42help[]
[role="arc42help"]
****
.Contents
A list of identified technical risks or technical debts, ordered by priority

.Motivation
“Risk management is project management for grown-ups” (Tim Lister, Atlantic Systems Guild.) 

This should be your motto for systematic detection and evaluation of risks and technical debts in the architecture, which will be needed by management stakeholders (e.g. project managers, product owners) as part of the overall risk analysis and measurement planning.

.Form
List of risks and/or technical debts, probably including suggested measures to minimize, mitigate or avoid risks or reduce technical debts.


.Further Information

****
endif::arc42help[]

The table below lists the most relevant technical risks and accepted
technical debts of *Drop*, ordered by impact. They are reviewed every
release cycle.

=== Risks

Risks are scored with *Probability × Impact* (each 1 – 3,
iSAQB LZ 9-3 / ISO 31000); priority: 1 – 2 monitor, 3 – 4 plan
mitigation, 6 – 9 mitigation mandatory.

[cols="1,4,1,1,1,5", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Risk | P | I | Score | Mitigation

| R1
| Battery and data consumption of live tracking on driver devices
  drives drivers to disable / uninstall the app and breaks matching.
| 3 | 2 | 6
| Tune update frequency in the Tracking Service, fall back to
  low-frequency mode on slow networks, expose a battery-saver mode.

| R2
| External map / routing provider outage or rate-limit hit degrades
  search and booking for all users.
| 2 | 3 | 6
| Circuit Breaker + Retry-with-Backoff, short-term route cache,
  RFC 7807 fail-fast so the app can show a clear message (QS-3).

| R3
| Event-bus backlog when a consumer is down causes Analytics and
  Notifications to lag by minutes to hours.
| 2 | 2 | 4
| Bounded retention, alerting on consumer lag, idempotent consumers
  so replays are safe.

| R4
| Stripe outage during peak hours delays receipts, refunds and
  payouts.
| 1 | 3 | 3
| Idempotent payment calls + retry; ride completion is decoupled
  from payment settlement (QS-3, ADR-3).
|===

=== Technical Debts

* *D1 – Analytics still in the monolith.* Acceptable until reporting
  load impacts operational latency; first extraction candidate.
* *D2 – Single-region deployment.* Sufficient for the Austrian
  rollout; revisit when the first non-EU country is added.
* *D3 – Manual approval between `staging` and `prod`.* Kept on
  purpose while the team is small; drop once end-to-end coverage is
  high enough to trust full continuous deployment.
