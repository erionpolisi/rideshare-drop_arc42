== Risks and Technical Debts

ifdef::arc42help[]
[role="arc42help"]
****
.Contents
A list of identified technical risks or technical debts, ordered by priority

.Motivation
“Risk management is project management for grown-ups” (Tim Lister, Atlantic Systems Guild.) 

This should be your motto for systematic detection and evaluation of risks and technical debts in the architecture, which will be needed by management stakeholders (e.g. project managers, product owners) as part of the overall risk analysis and measurement planning.

.Form
List of risks and/or technical debts, probably including suggested measures to minimize, mitigate or avoid risks or reduce technical debts.


.Further Information

See https://docs.arc42.org/section-11/[Risks and Technical Debt] in the arc42 documentation.

****
endif::arc42help[]

The table below lists the most relevant technical risks and accepted
technical debts of *Drop*, ordered by impact. They are reviewed every
release cycle.

=== Risks

[cols="1,2,3,3", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Risk | Impact | Mitigation

| R1
| Battery and data consumption of live tracking on driver devices
| Drivers may disable the app or uninstall it, breaking the matching process.
| Tune the update frequency of the Realtime Service; fall back to lower-frequency updates on slow networks.

| R2
| External map / routing provider outage or rate-limit hit
| Search and booking degrade for all users.
| Circuit Breaker + Retry-with-Backoff; short-term cache of recent routes; fail fast with RFC 7807 error so the app can show
a clear message.

| R3
| Driver-verification provider unavailable
| New drivers cannot be onboarded.
| Verification can fall back to a manual in-house process so onboarding continues.
|===

=== Technical Debts

[cols="1,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Debt | Plan

| D1
| Monolith deploys the Analytics context together with the operational contexts.
| Acceptable until reporting load impacts operational latency; extract Analytics first when that happens.

| D2
| No multi-region active/active deployment.
| Sufficient for the initial Austrian rollout. Revisit when the first country outside the EU is added.

| D3
| Manual approval step between `staging` and `prod` in CI/CD.
| Kept on purpose while the team is small to avoid unattended bad deploys; revisit once automated end-to-end coverage is high enough to
trust full continuous deployment.
|===
