== Risks and Technical Debts

The table below lists the most relevant technical risks and accepted
technical debts of *Drop*, ordered by impact. They are reviewed every
release cycle.

=== Risks

Risks are evaluated with the standard *Probability × Impact* matrix
inspired by ISO 31000:2018 and recommended by the iSAQB Foundation
syllabus (LZ 9-3). Each axis uses three levels:

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Level | Probability | Impact

| Low (1)
| Unlikely to occur within one release cycle.
| Minor user impact, easy to recover, no data loss.

| Medium (2)
| Plausible during a release cycle.
| Several users affected, manual recovery, no data loss.

| High (3)
| Expected to occur unless mitigated.
| Outage of a critical feature, regulatory or financial impact.
|===

The *Score* is `Probability × Impact` and drives the priority:
1 – 2 monitor, 3 – 4 plan mitigation, 6 – 9 mitigation is mandatory.

[cols="1,3,1,1,1,4,4", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Risk | P | I | Score | Impact (details) | Mitigation

| R1
| Battery and data consumption of live tracking on driver devices
| 3 | 2 | 6
| Drivers may disable the app or uninstall it, breaking the matching process.
| Tune the update frequency of the Tracking Service; fall back to lower-frequency updates on slow networks; expose a battery-saver mode.

| R2
| External map / routing provider outage or rate-limit hit
| 2 | 3 | 6
| Search and booking degrade for all users.
| Circuit Breaker + Retry-with-Backoff; short-term cache of recent routes; fail fast with RFC 7807 error so the app can show a clear message.

| R3
| Driver-verification provider unavailable
| 2 | 2 | 4
| New drivers cannot be onboarded.
| Verification can fall back to a manual in-house process so onboarding continues.

| R4
| Stripe outage during peak hours
| 1 | 3 | 3
| Rides complete but receipts are delayed; refunds and payouts are queued.
| Idempotent payment calls + retry; ride completion is decoupled from payment settlement (QS-3).

| R5
| Event-bus (Kafka / Pub/Sub) backlog when a consumer is down
| 2 | 2 | 4
| Analytics and Notifications lag behind by minutes to hours.
| Bounded retention; alerting on consumer lag; consumers are idempotent so replays are safe.

| R6
| GDPR / DSGVO violation through over-retention of location data
| 1 | 3 | 3
| Regulatory fine, reputational damage, mandatory disclosure.
| Hard 24-hour retention on Firestore live-location documents; quarterly audit of data-retention rules (constraint OC1).
|===

=== Technical Debts

[cols="1,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Debt | Plan

| D1
| Monolith deploys the Analytics context together with the operational contexts.
| Acceptable until reporting load impacts operational latency; extract Analytics first when that happens.

| D2
| No multi-region active/active deployment.
| Sufficient for the initial Austrian rollout. Revisit when the first country outside the EU is added.

| D3
| Manual approval step between `staging` and `prod` in CI/CD.
| Kept on purpose while the team is small to avoid unattended bad deploys; revisit once automated end-to-end coverage is high enough to
trust full continuous deployment.
|===
